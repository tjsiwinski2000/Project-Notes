Two ways of using boto3 to access dynamo DB

DIRECTLY AS A RESOURCE
 dynamodb = boto3.resource('dynamodb', region_name=str(os.environ['REGION_NAME']))

SECOND WAY
dynamodb = boto3.client('dynamodb')
table_name = str(os.environ['DYNAMODB_TABLE'])
res = dynamodb.put_item(
  TableName=table_name,
  #NOTE: conversion required see dynamo.py
  Item=dynamo.to_item(post)
)
