##AI agents and chatbots##
 serve different purposes
 distinction is becoming less clear
 
 Traditional chatbots generally
 - limited to predefined scripts and specific tasks

 AI agents 
 - can perform complex operations 
 - interact intelligently with other applications.
 - SIMPLE DEF: an LLM that can run tools
 
### example ###
ChatGPT is pushing the boundary by incorporating functionalities typically associated with AI applications, such as scheduling tasks and sending emails directly. This evolution creates a gray area, as models like ChatGPT blur the lines between simple chatbots and fully-fledged AI agents by adding functionalities that allow for more sophisticated interactions.

## RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation ##
Enhances AI capabilities by integrating retrieval mechanisms 
with large language models (LLMs).

The AI can generate responses 
- not only based on its pre-trained knowledge 
- but ALSO by retrieving relevant information from external datasets, such as user-specific data.
